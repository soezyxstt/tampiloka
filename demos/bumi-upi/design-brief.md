Revision date: 2026-10-23. Applying prospect-to-website-demo 0.4.1. Parent user approved cloud-workspace build/QA override; no deployment/outreach.

Olive makes the opening belong to Bumi Upi; the following near-white menu pages provide a deliberate change in density. The original rounded raster logo remains unchanged. This replaces the current generic cream/Georgia split hero and repeated sentimental copy.

Palette confidence medium, composition adaptation deliberate, source freshness limited to the archive.

## Decisions

Opening: one h1, broad low-contrast sans appropriate to archived menu hierarchy; no invented slogan. Images: varied rectangular prints; original watermarks visible and no arch masks. Surfaces: olive opening, pale neutral reading sheets, light green location. Catalogue: 116 unmodified item records; eight items/page replaces previous subset; category Select is the single category control. Price/variant groups stay together. Small authentic thumbnails respect source resolution. Entire catalogue remains available through category selection, pagination, and search. Group metadata: one price availability note and daily-prasmanan note. No fabricated service time for breakfast.

Copy: keep identity, address, hours, menu names/descriptions/prices/variants, photobooth fact, daily menu note and contact. Remove transferable sentiments about home/unfinished conversation and duplicate descriptions. Heading Rumah tamah comes from official identity; headings Buku menu, Di dalam atau di halaman, and Mampir ke rumah are functional navigation/context.

Motion: album prints settle once, pagination slides like leaf changes, memory photo changes crossfade/rotate, mobile search expands horizontally; reduced-motion disables offsets, rotations and smooth scroll. Existing Base UI Select/Button/Input remain intact. No pills, chips, badges or overlines. Google iframe retains controls/attribution and directions fallback.

**Room and memory album** — schulhaus_07, template-whisk-03

Mechanism: Image scale contrast followed by an interactive stack of photo prints. Rejected: Long editorial prose, coral palette and invented testimonials; reference interactions are not verified. Mobile: Wide room first, short factual heading next, then album beneath its action.

**Location** — template-mello-02

Mechanism: Restrained light/green surface transition gives the final practical information its own space. Adaptation: Exact archived address, hours, WhatsApp, actual facade and Google Maps iframe. Rejected: Decorative motif strips and unrelated mood-match feature. Mobile: Address and hours above full-width map and fallback direction link.

## Refinement 2026-11-10

Penawaran sarapan/makan/minum dan dua aksi kini berada sebelum album. Buku menu menjadi enam data per spread, tiga per leaf, dengan akses semua 116 data. Album dan galeri mobile dipadatkan; foto booth tidak diulang di pembuka.

Identitas, aset dan data sumber arsip dipertahankan. Native anchors dan shadcn/Base UI controls tetap digunakan; panah pagination/galeri tetap fungsional. Motion gambar CSS hanya pada layar besar yang mendukung scroll timeline; fallback tetap terlihat, reveal mobile statis. Tidak ada fakta, harga atau testimoni baru. Referensi WF-08 menu ledger dilihat pada desktop/mobile untuk hubungan nama-harga dan garis pembatas, bukan untuk menyalin palet, serif, copy atau struktur seluruh halaman.
