# Local fonts

Active: Poppins Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600), Bold (700), downloaded from the official Google Fonts repository, https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/poppins. License: poppins-OFL.txt. Loaded through next/font/local.

The official Historia menu embeds Poppins Regular/Medium/Bold for category headings, product names, prices and supporting labels. Quiche Display appears in descriptions and the cover; its embedded subsets are not deployed as webfonts. No Quiche webfont license was supplied.

Earlier Inter, Manrope and Instrument Serif files are retained as iteration history but are not loaded by this version.
