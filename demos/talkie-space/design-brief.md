## Decisions

Opening: factual “Coffee & Roastery.” in source-like heavy sans at broad scale; no generic slogan or overline. Image frame: square corners, black gutters, coffee dominant, brewing and breakfast small. Surface: dark majority, one pale breakfast information band as supported by white food background. Catalogue: 20 meaningful categories, 8 items per replacing page, cross-category search; true category photos separate from exact SKU photos. Price and name remain close. Metadata (PPN and availability) appears once. Header is static and catalogue sticky ends with catalogue. Copy: functional service periods, prices, place/contact; remove generic ‘ambil jeda’ and ‘sampai bertemu’ headings. Motion: coffee image settles once, category results move subtly on change, mobile search expands; reduced motion removes transforms/durations. Controls reuse existing official shadcn/Base UI tabs/button/input.

## Refinement 2026-11-10

Kopi tetap fokus utama workbench gelap. Aksi menu lebih jelas; mobile mengganti rail foto sekunder dengan dua tautan konteks, mengurangi foto duplikat, dan menampilkan enam hasil katalog per halaman. Seluruh 94 data dipertahankan.

Identitas, aset dan data sumber arsip dipertahankan. Native anchors dan shadcn/Base UI controls tetap digunakan; panah pagination/galeri tetap fungsional. Motion gambar CSS hanya pada layar besar yang mendukung scroll timeline; fallback tetap terlihat, reveal mobile statis. Tidak ada fakta, harga atau testimoni baru.
